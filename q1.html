Are there months or seasons when road crash hospitalisations peak?

About this view

Each bar shows the total road crash hospitalisations for a calendar month, added up across 2011–2021. The orange bar marks the month with the highest total. Hover over a bar to see its exact count.

Each bar shows the total road crash hospitalisations for an Australian season, added up across 2011–2021. Summer is December to February, Autumn is March to May, Winter is June to August and Spring is September to November. The orange bar marks the season with the highest total.

Key findings

  • March has the highest monthly total: 36,813 hospitalisations.
  • June has the lowest: 32,470, a difference of 4,343.
  • Autumn (March to May) has the highest seasonal total: 106,109.
  • Winter has the lowest: 97,887, which is 8,222 fewer than Autumn.
