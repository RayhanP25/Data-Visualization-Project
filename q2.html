How does the gap between males and females change across age groups and road user types?

Male Female

About this view

Each pair of bars shows male and female road crash hospitalisations for one age group, added up across 2011–2021. Hover over a pair to see the counts and the gap between them.

Each row shows one road user group. The navy dot is males and the orange dot is females, and the line between them is the gap. Longer lines mean a bigger gap in counts, not a bigger share.

Key findings

  • The 40–64 age group has the largest gap: 44,132 more male hospitalisations.
  • Males are higher in 6 of the 7 age groups.
  • The 75+ group is the only one with more female hospitalisations: 63 more.
  • Motorcyclists have the largest gap: 73,062 more male hospitalisations.
  • Pedal cyclists are next, with 42,922 more male hospitalisations.
  • Car passengers are the largest female-higher group: 10,488 more female hospitalisations.
