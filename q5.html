How did 2020 (the COVID-19 year) differ from the 2011–2019 trend, and which road user groups changed most?

About this view

Each bar shows how far 2020 hospitalisations for a road user group were from what the 2011–2019 trend predicted. Orange bars fell below expected and blue bars rose above it. Darker shades mean bigger changes. Use the Road users menu to show or hide groups.

Each bar shows the number of hospitalisations above (+) or below (−) what the 2011–2019 trend predicted for 2020. Use the Road users menu to show or hide groups.

Only road user groups with more than 500 hospitalisations a year are shown, because smaller groups swing too much from year to year to support conclusions.

Key findings

  • Pedestrians fell furthest, 21.7% below expected.
  • Pedal cyclists were the only group above expected, up 8.6%.
  • Car passengers (−16.2%) and car drivers (−14.4%) also fell sharply.
  • Overall, 36,411 hospitalisations against 39,710 expected, down 8.3%.
