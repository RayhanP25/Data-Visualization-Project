Which road user groups increased or decreased the most from 2011 to 2021?

About this view

Each bar shows how much a road user group's hospitalisations changed from 2011 to 2021, as a percentage of its 2011 count. Navy bars rose and orange-red bars fell. Turn on "Show cases" to see the change in cases instead.

Each line starts at 100 in 2011. A line above 100 means the group has grown since 2011, and a line below 100 means it has shrunk. Every group is on the same scale, whatever its size.

Use the Road users menu to show or hide groups.

Key findings

  • Pedal cyclists rose the most in cases: +2,772 (+51.4%).
  • Heavy transport unknown position had the largest percentage fall: −34.5%, from 55 to 36 cases.
  • Pedestrians had the largest fall in cases: −420 (−15.3%).
  • Car drivers added 2,379 cases (+21.7%).
